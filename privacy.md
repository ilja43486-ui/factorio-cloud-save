# Privacy policy - Factorio Cloud Save

Factorio Cloud Save is a desktop helper used by a private group of friends to share their Factorio game saves through Google Drive.

What the app accesses: only the Google Drive folder that the players share for their Factorio saves. In that folder it lists, uploads, downloads, renames and deletes game save files, game mods, program updates and small status files (for example, which player is currently in the game).

Where data is stored: the Google sign-in token is stored only on the player's own computer, in the configuration of the rclone tool that the app uses. Save files and mods are stored in the players' own Google Drive folder. The token and files are never sent to the developer or to any third party. The app has no server of its own and collects no analytics or other personal data.

How to revoke access: at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, or by deleting the app and its folder from the computer.

Contact: antonykireevi@gmail.com

# Политика конфиденциальности

Программа работает только с общей папкой сейвов Factorio на Google Диске игроков: выгружает, скачивает, переименовывает и удаляет сейвы, моды, обновления программы и небольшие служебные файлы. Ключ входа в Google хранится только на компьютере игрока и никому не передаётся. Своего сервера у программы нет, никакие данные не собираются. Отозвать доступ можно в любой момент: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
